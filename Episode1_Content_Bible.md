## Thumbnail Concepts

**Concept A — "Calculated"**
- Layout: Split composition — left half: elegant Victorian dinner table (candles, white cloth); right half: dirt floor with a shovel. Hard diagonal cut between the two halves.
- Text Overlay: `SHE PLANNED THIS` — heavy white serif caps, bottom-left, outlined in black
- Expression: No face — the contrast does the emotional work
- Colors: Deep burgundy and candlelight gold (left) / cold grey-black (right)
- Hook Element: Visual juxtaposition of dinner party and grave — viewer's brain has to reconcile the two images

**Concept B — "Witness" (Recommended)**

- Text Overlay: `50,000 WATCHED HER DIE` in bold red capitals across the top; her name in smaller white text bottom-right
- Expression: Eerily calm — contrast between her composure and the execution crowd
- Colors: Sepia/parchment background, red text, dark vignette edges
- Hook Element: Eye contact from a historical subject plus the crowd number
