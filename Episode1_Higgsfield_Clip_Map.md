# Higgsfield Prompts — Killer Kontent Ep.1: The Bermondsey Horror

Each prompt maps directly to one section so you can generate them in order and cut to the narration timing.

---

**HOOK (0:00 – 0:45)**
> Cinematic dark Victorian London streets at night, cobblestones wet with rain, gas lamps glowing amber, slow dramatic push forward. Cut to an elegantly set candlelit dinner table with white linen, silverware, and two wine glasses. Cut to a dark stone kitchen floor, shadows, a single candle flickering. Moody, ominous atmosphere. Film grain. 4K cinematic.

---

**ACT 1 — WHO WAS MARIA? (0:45 – 3:00)**
> Wide establishing shot of Victorian London in daytime, horse-drawn carriages, crowded market streets, smog over rooftops. Cut to the exterior of a grand English townhouse, ivy-covered brick, iron railings. Cut to a close-up of a black and white maid's uniform laid neatly on a bed. Atmospheric, period-accurate, cinematic grade.

---

**ACT 2 — THE PLAN (3:00 – 5:30)**
> Close-up of a dirty shovel being placed against a stone wall, soil on the blade. Cut to heavy hessian sacks of white powder (quicklime) stacked in a dark cellar. Cut to an elegant handwritten dinner invitation on aged cream paper, candlelight casting shadows across the ink. Slow, deliberate camera movement. Dark Victorian aesthetic.

---

**ACT 3 — THE GETAWAY (5:30 – 7:30)**
> Wide shot of a busy Victorian train station, steam billowing across the platform, crowds rushing. Cut to a woman in a dark travelling coat and bonnet walking briskly, carpetbag in hand, not looking back. Cut to a lone man slumped at a dimly lit bar, a large glass of brandy in front of him, looking dishevelled. Cinematic, dramatic lighting, period accurate.

---

**ACT 4 — THE TRIAL (7:30 – 9:00)**
> Interior of a packed Victorian courtroom, the Old Bailey, wooden gallery filled with spectators in period dress, gas lamps overhead. Cut to close-up of newspaper front pages spread across a table, dramatic ink headlines. Tense atmosphere, tight framing, muted colour palette.

---

**ACT 5 — THE EXECUTION (9:00 – 10:30)**
> Aerial wide shot of a vast crowd of 50,000 people packed around a stone prison building at dawn, London skyline in background, mist rising. Cut to rooftops with spectators craning to see, some holding monoculars. Cut to dawn light breaking over Horsemonger Lane Gaol, dramatic golden-grey sky. Epic scale, slow cinematic pan.

---

**LEGACY (10:30 – 11:30)**
> Close-up of an aged copy of Bleak House by Charles Dickens, leather-bound, pages worn. Slow zoom in on the cover. Cut to a vintage ink illustration of a Victorian woman, cold expression, high collar. Warm candlelight, shallow depth of field, cinematic grain.

---

**OUTRO (11:30 – END)**
